Single Wills

A Single Will is a Will made by one person — whether you're single, divorced, widowed, or simply want your own arrangements set out separately from a partner. It gives you full control over who inherits your estate and who cares for your children.

As we're based in Buckingham, we serve clients across Buckinghamshire and Oxfordshire through home visits and remote video or phone consultations — whichever suits you best. Wherever you're most comfortable meeting, the advice and attention to detail stay exactly the same.

Every Will we prepare includes unlimited changes to your draft, a printed version delivered to you, and a clause clarification guide so you understand exactly what each part of your Will means. There's no pressure and no jargon — just clear, honest advice at your own pace.

  • Decide exactly who inherits your money, property and possessions
  • Appoint guardians for children under 18
  • Choose your executors and record your wishes clearly
  • Reduce stress, delay and potential disputes for your family
