Privacy Policy

How AJ Wills & Estate Planning collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Introduction

AJ Wills & Estate Planning ('we', 'us', 'our') is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, and details you share with us relating to Will Writing, Lasting Powers of Attorney or Will Storage enquiries — whether via our contact form, chatbot, or guide download requests.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, arrange consultations, provide our services, and — where you've consented — send you relevant guidance and updates.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. Information may be shared with trusted third parties strictly where necessary to deliver our services, such as a solicitor partner for complex referrals, or our secure document storage provider.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and to withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time.

Contact Us

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at amy@ajwills.uk.
