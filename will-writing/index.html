Protect the People Who Matter Most

Why Make a Will

Making a Will is one of the most important things you can do for your family. It gives you control over who inherits your estate, who will look after your children, and helps ensure your wishes are carried out after your death.

Without a valid Will, your estate will be distributed according to the Rules of Intestacy, which may not reflect your wishes. At AJ Wills & Estate Planning, we provide straightforward, professional Will writing with clear advice, whether you prefer to meet online or in the comfort of your own home.

  • Choose exactly who inherits your money, property and possessions
  • Appoint guardians for children under 18
  • Provide for step-children, unmarried partners or dependants who have no automatic entitlement
  • Minimise the risk of family disputes and unnecessary delay
Without a Will

What Happens Without a Will

If you die without a valid Will, you die "intestate" — and the law, not you, decides what happens to your estate.

Fixed Legal Rules Apply

The rules of intestacy follow a strict order — spouse, children, parents, siblings — regardless of your actual wishes.

Unmarried Partners Miss Out

Unmarried partners have no automatic right to inherit under intestacy rules, no matter how long you've been together.

Delay and Uncertainty

Without a named executor, the process of administering your estate can take significantly longer.

Types of Wills

Every family is different — we'll recommend the right type of Will for your specific circumstances.

Single Wills

A straightforward Will for one person, covering your assets, beneficiaries and executors.

Mirror Wills

Matching Wills for couples, typically leaving everything to one another and then to shared beneficiaries.

Trust Based Wills

Wills that incorporate trusts to protect assets for children, vulnerable beneficiaries or future generations.

Complex Family Wills

Tailored solutions for blended families, business owners and more complex estates.
