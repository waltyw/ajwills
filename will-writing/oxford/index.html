Will Writing Oxford

A clear, professionally drafted Will gives you control over who inherits your estate, who cares for your children, and ensures your wishes are carried out — exactly as you intend. We provide plain-English Will writing in Oxford and the surrounding area.

As we're based in Buckingham, we serve clients in Oxford and the surrounding area through home visits and remote video or phone consultations — whichever suits you best. Wherever you're most comfortable meeting, the advice and attention to detail stay exactly the same.

Every Will we prepare includes unlimited changes to your draft, a printed version delivered to you, and a clause clarification guide so you understand exactly what each part of your Will means. There's no pressure and no jargon — just clear, honest advice at your own pace, from your very first enquiry through to signing.

  • Decide exactly who inherits your money, property and possessions
  • Appoint guardians for children under 18
  • Reduce stress, delay and potential disputes for your family
  • Support tax-efficient estate planning where appropriate

A Mirror Will is where two people — usually partners or spouses — each make a Will that mirrors the other, typically leaving everything to one another and then to the same beneficiaries, such as shared children. It keeps both documents consistent while still allowing each person to make their own individual choices where needed.
Yes — a Will is the only formal way to name legal guardians for children under 18 should the unexpected happen. Without this in place, the court decides based on what it believes is in your children's best interests, which may not reflect your own wishes, so naming your preferred guardians in writing gives your family clarity at an already difficult time.
