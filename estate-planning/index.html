Estate Planning

Protecting your family's future takes more than just a Will. Our estate planning service brings together Will writing, Lasting Powers of Attorney and secure document storage, so every part of your plan works together instead of being handled piecemeal by different providers.

As we're based in Buckingham, we serve clients across Buckinghamshire and Oxfordshire through home visits and remote video or phone consultations — whichever suits you best. Wherever you're most comfortable meeting, the advice and attention to detail stay exactly the same.

Whether you're starting from scratch or already have some documents in place, we'll take the time to understand your family's circumstances and recommend exactly what's needed — nothing more, nothing less. Every recommendation is explained in plain English, with fixed, transparent fees agreed upfront, so there are never any surprises further down the line.

  • Professionally drafted Wills tailored to your family
  • Lasting Powers of Attorney for property and health decisions
  • Secure storage for your original documents
  • Guidance on trusts and reducing Inheritance Tax where appropriate
