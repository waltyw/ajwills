Will Reviews

Life moves on, and your Will should move with it. Whether it was written by us, another provider, or some years ago, a professional review checks your existing Will still says what you want it to — and still qualifies as legally valid.

As we're based in Buckingham, we review Wills for clients across Buckinghamshire and Oxfordshire through home visits and remote video or phone consultations — whichever suits you best. Common reasons to review a Will include marriage, divorce, a new child or grandchild, moving house, or a change in your assets.

We'll go through your existing Will with you in plain English, explain anything that's out of date or missing, and recommend exactly what's needed — whether that's a simple update or a new Will altogether — with a fixed fee agreed upfront.

  • Check your Will still reflects your current wishes
  • Confirm it remains correctly signed, witnessed and valid
  • Identify any gaps, such as missing guardianship provisions
  • Recommend updates or a new Will where needed
