Will Storage Buckinghamshire

A professionally drafted Will is only half the job — keeping the original safe and easy to find matters just as much. We provide secure Will storage across Buckinghamshire, for £50 per year. Looking for safe will storage in Buckinghamshire?

As we're based in Buckingham, we serve clients across Buckinghamshire through home visits and remote video or phone consultations — whichever suits you best. Wherever you're most comfortable meeting, the advice and attention to detail stay exactly the same.

You'll receive a storage certificate with clear instructions for your executors, so there's never any doubt about where to find it when it's needed most, whether that's in ten years or considerably sooner.

A professionally stored Will is protected from loss, damage, fire and theft, and can always be located quickly by your executors — avoiding delay or dispute at an already difficult time for your family. It also means your Will cannot be accidentally destroyed or misplaced during a house move.
Wills kept at home can be lost, damaged, misplaced during a house move, or simply not found by family members when needed. Secure storage removes that risk entirely, and gives you a single, reliable place your executors can always turn to.
