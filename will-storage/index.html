Something to Consider

Risks of Keeping a Will at Home

Many people keep their Will in a drawer, filing cabinet or home safe. While this may seem convenient, problems can arise if nobody knows where it's kept, it's accidentally thrown away, damaged by fire or water, lost during a house move, or simply cannot be found when your executors need it most. Without the original Will, administering an estate can become significantly more complicated.

Fire, Flood & Theft

Household disasters, flooding or theft can destroy or remove the only copy of your Will, leaving your family without clear instructions.

Simply Not Found

Family members may not know where to look, or may not find it at all amongst other paperwork.

House Moves & Clear-Outs

Important documents are easily misplaced or accidentally thrown away during a house move or clear-out.
