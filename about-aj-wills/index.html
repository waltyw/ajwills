Meet the Founder

Who We Are

Hello, I'm Amy, the founder of AJ Wills.

I've always believed that planning for the future is one of the most important things we can do for the people we love. As a wife and mum, I understand how important it is to know that your family will be looked after and that your wishes are clearly set out.

My journey into estate planning began with a passion for law. I graduated with a First Class Honours Law Degree before completing specialist training in professional Will writing. Combining my legal knowledge with a friendly, approachable style allows me to help clients make informed decisions without confusing legal jargon.

Before founding AJ Wills, I successfully ran my own business for a number of years, where I built lasting relationships with clients and developed a reputation for professionalism, reliability and exceptional customer service. Those same values are at the heart of everything I do today.

I created AJ Wills because I wanted to offer a personal, approachable service where clients feel listened to, understood and supported. Every family is different, and I believe every Will and Lasting Power of Attorney should reflect each person's individual circumstances, values and wishes.

Whether you're making your very first Will, updating an existing one, or putting Lasting Powers of Attorney in place, my aim is to make the process straightforward, professional and reassuring from beginning to end.

Thank you for considering AJ Wills. It would be a privilege to help you protect what matters most and give you and your loved ones peace of mind for the future.

Giving Back

Proud to Support Annabelle's Challenge

At AJ Wills, we believe in protecting families — not only through professional estate planning, but by supporting causes that make a real difference.

We are proud to support Annabelle's Challenge, a charity dedicated to raising awareness of vascular Ehlers-Danlos syndrome (vEDS), funding research, and providing support to individuals and families affected by this rare condition.

Supporting Annabelle's Challenge reflects the values at the heart of AJ Wills: protecting families, planning for the future, and helping to make a positive difference in the lives of others. We are honoured to stand alongside Annabelle's Challenge and encourage our clients and visitors to learn more about their incredible work.
